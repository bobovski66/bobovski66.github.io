A little orientation

Start with a factory patch and play the keyboard. Then change a source or move the morph slider while holding a note.

Two sources, one sound

A and B each produce a sampled waveform. The morph slider blends their amplitudes point by point. Source shapes are normalized individually; cancellation between them is part of the sound. Capture blend turns the result into material you can reshape.

Play an address

The sequencer builds a finite word in base 2, 3, or 4. Read as a number and divided by the largest word of that length, it gives a coordinate u between 0 and 1. The same coordinate chooses a scale note, controls the selected sequence targets, and highlights a path in the address tree. A and B also show their own fractal-specific addresses at that coordinate. These may use different alphabets.

Keep what you find

Save patch stores both sources, envelopes, effects, morph position, and sequence in this browser. Export a patch or bank to carry it elsewhere. Custom sampled regions travel with the patch. Your current edits also recover after reloading; audio always starts stopped.

Take the sound elsewhere

Wavetable WAV exports a single mono cycle at 48 kHz; a 2,048-sample cycle has a reference pitch of 23.4375 Hz when played directly. Wavetable JSON includes samples and Fourier coefficients. Sequence WAV renders two passes using the same voice and effects, with a tail for release and echoes. Download the whole instrument for offline use.

These are finite sampled realizations and sonifications. A plotted geometric set, the scan used to make its waveform, and its sound are related views, not identical objects. No network or MIDI connection is needed to play.
