A little orientation

Root clouds

Choose a family of polynomials and plot every root of every chosen polynomial. When a finite family fits within the budget, the explorer enumerates it completely. Larger or continuous families use a repeatable seeded sample, balancing degrees where their family sizes permit. The default includes every degree-2, -3, and -4 polynomial with integer coefficients in [−2, 2], with nonzero leading and constant terms.

A nonzero constant term excludes z = 0. A bounded discrete coefficient set can also leave a visible neighborhood around zero empty. Change the coefficient family to see which patterns persist. Clouds from real coefficients are conjugation-symmetric; arbitrary complex coefficients need not be.

Coefficients

The leading coefficient is fixed at 1. Edit real and imaginary parts, drag the selected coefficient in its small complex plane, or drag a root in the main plot. Root dragging reconstructs the polynomial from its linear factors. Select a cloud point and open it here to manipulate the same roots.

Root braids

Hold Q fixed and solve Q(z) − c = 0 while c follows a closed path. Local root labels are continued along the path using adaptive subdivision and a minimum-cost assignment. The endpoint permutation tells you which starting location receives each label. In the braid view, the third coordinate is progress along the loop; drag to rotate it.

Try Two turns · a pure braid. Each root comes home, yet the discriminant winds twice. Thus the permutation alone does not capture the whole braid. A loop that crosses a branch value has a collision and is rejected.

Numerical meaning

Root solutions are checked both by a scaled polynomial residual and by reconstructing their coefficients. These are numerical checks, not certified error bounds. Multiple or very close roots are ill-conditioned and are flagged. A continuation that does not resolve its labels is marked uncertain rather than presented as a reliable permutation.

Cloud generation and braid tracing run in background workers. Saved experiments retain the coefficient family, sample seed, polynomial, and loop; the data are regenerated when opened. The explorer needs no account or external library. The original gallery images remain online assets.
