Case Briefing1 / 2

What crossed over?

Investigators are here to find which paranormal entities are responsible for the lights, cries, disappearances, and geometric distortions spreading through the Barrens.

Witnesses disagree completely. Reports conflict: a winged cryptid, blank-faced visitors, voices with no bodies, missing minutes, predatory geometry, and lights that imitate people. The map will not stay stable for long enough to learn the truth from a distance.

Your assignment

Enter the zone, gather evidence, identify the pattern, and stabilize the portals before local reality gives up.

Not every manifestation should be fought. Some things only stalk, unsettle, or draw Exposure. Keep your team alive and sane, collect six evidence, and then close every portal you can find.

Core Loop

Collect 6 evidence, identify the phenomenon, then stabilize every open portal before Doom reaches 15.

Turn

  • Each active investigator gets 2 actions. You act first; AI teammates follow. Then the paranormal side moves, attacks, spawns, checks Doom, and resolves weirdness before showing its summary.
  • Click rooms, doors, clues, caches, portals, investigators, or manifestations directly. The ACTIONS drawer shows only legal actions for the selected target.

Rooms & Movement

  • Rooms have 2–4 visible doors. A ? door explores exactly one adjacent new room. A normal Move action crosses a number of connected, already-discovered rooms equal to the investigator’s Move stat. Move is distance per action, not extra actions. Most investigators have Move 1; Samir has Move 2, so one action can carry him up to 2 known rooms. You may enter a contested room but never move through one.
  • Doom weirdness can move or erase doors and safe empty rooms, but cannot strand investigators from required evidence or open portals.
  • A room containing a red HOSTILE is contested. Until every hostile there is cleared, investigators cannot leave, collect evidence, search a field cache, or stabilize a portal. They may still attack, defend, use powers or gear, regroup, support allies, or end their turn.

Manifestations & Combat

  • CONTACTS follow investigators and attempt phenomenon-specific Nerve/Exposure effects. Unless Protective Mask blocks the effect first, make a Contact Check: d6 + current Nerve vs 9. A total of 9 or more resists the effect. At high Doom, a contact that catches its target has a small chance to become HOSTILE.
  • Contact limit: during one paranormal phase, an investigator can lose at most 1 Nerve and gain at most 1 Exposure from CONTACTS. Additional contacts may still follow, convert to HOSTILE, or attempt an effect that has not yet reached its cap.
  • HOSTILES attack, lock exits, and block portal stabilization. Basic Attack may target any manifestation; powers and gear obey their own target restrictions.
  • Hostile cap: no room can contain more than 3 HOSTILES. At 3, portal spawns in that room can only create CONTACTS, existing CONTACTS cannot turn HOSTILE, and additional HOSTILES cannot enter until the count drops below 3.
  • Attack with d6 + Combat vs Defense. If the total exceeds Defense, damage equals the margin: Attack total − Defense. A tie deals no damage. Defend costs 1 action for +2 Defense until your next turn. Regroup costs 1 action once per round for +1 Nerve.
  • Health 0 stuns an investigator for 2 rounds. Nerve 0 or Exposure 6 stuns them for 1 round. After the required rounds are missed, the stat that caused the stun fully recovers: Health returns to full, Nerve returns to full, or Exposure resets to 0.
  • Identical manifestations with the same target/state may display as one ×N group; they remain separate creatures mechanically.

Evidence & Portals

  • Evidence 1–4 updates the hypotheses. Evidence 5 identifies the phenomenon. Evidence 6 completes the case evidence and triggers The Threshold Reacts: one open portal immediately produces a manifestation.
  • Evidence frequency depends on Case Pressure: Story 50%, Standard 40%, Severe 30% per newly explored room. To prevent long dry spells, a clue is forced after 3, 4, or 5 consecutive clue-less rooms respectively.
  • AI evidence priority: if an AI investigator is in a room with accessible evidence, it investigates before taking any other action. It will never leave usable evidence behind. A HOSTILE contesting the room is the only exception.
  • Every case begins with one portal and guarantees another during the first three explorations. After that, a newly explored room opens a portal with probability 8% Story, 13% Standard, or 18% Severe. After 6 evidence, click a portal to Stabilize it. A contested room must be cleared first.

Gear

  • Each investigator carries up to 3 gear cards. Click a gear chip on a Party card to see its use. Some field gear now carries multiple charges, shown directly on the card.
  • Repel gear is deliberately common: Bear Spray and Rock Salt have multiple copies, and repel-oriented equipment uses 3 charges before it is exhausted. Rare breakout cards such as Salt Bomb and Flash Charge can decisively clear a contested room.
  • Mobility gear: Trail GPS has 3 charges and adds +1 Move for one travel action; Emergency Beacon has 3 charges and moves up to 2 rooms toward a teammate; Lightweight Field Pack is passive and gives +1 Move on the holder’s first Move action each turn. None of these explore new rooms or bypass a hostile lock.
  • Field cache: choose which field-team investigator receives the card. Manifestation drop: the investigator who defeated it picks the card up immediately.
  • AI investigators use their powers, consumables, and passive gear when their conditions are useful.

Doom & Difficulty

  • Doom can only increase during the paranormal phase. The base pressure-check chance is 30% Story, 55% Standard, or 80% Severe; party size and Faraday Pouch modify it. Gear may reduce Doom during investigator turns. At higher Doom, spatial weirdness and contact escalation become more likely.
  • During each paranormal phase, every open portal has a base 16% Story, 24% Standard, or 38% Severe chance to produce a manifestation. Party size and Portal Snare modify that chance.
  • Story: accessible evidence and fewer portals. Standard: spread-out evidence and moderate portal pressure. Severe: sparse evidence with frequent portals and much higher hostile/Doom pressure.
  • Party size also scales pressure: solo receives reduced pressure and +1 Stabilize; duo receives lightly reduced pressure; trio receives increased pressure. Enemy Health and Defense do not scale.
