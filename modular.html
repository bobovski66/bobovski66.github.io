Behind the geometry

Start: z = eiθ
End: zc = eicθ (integer c)
Chord: i → (c · i) mod N

For multiplier 2, the chords trace a cardioid envelope. Its cusp is opposite the fixed-point angle.

For integer c ≥ 2, the ideal continuous envelope is E(θ) = (c eiθ + eicθ)/(c + 1). The c − 1 cusps occur halfway between neighboring fixed-point angles. For noninteger c, the chosen angle representatives do not define a continuous circle map.

Tip: Space plays/pauses; left/right arrows adjust c; Shift + arrows make fine adjustments. Animation wraps at c = 10,000. All calculations run locally in your browser.
